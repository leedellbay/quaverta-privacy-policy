Quaverta — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This is a template describing how Quaverta actually works, based on a review of the app's code and dependencies. It is not legal advice — have a lawyer review it before relying on it for a store listing.

Quaverta ("the app") is a construction estimating and project-takeoff tool published by Lee Dell Bay Innovations LLC ("we", "us"). The app is paid and has no ads, no user account, no backend server, and no analytics or telemetry. It is not fully offline, though: two specific, user-initiated features reach the network, described in full in Section 4 below — everything else described in this policy reflects an app that runs entirely on-device.

1. Information the app collects

Everything the app stores comes directly from what you type or photograph while using it:

  • Project details you enter: name, client, address, and notes.
  • Structure dimensions and openings you enter for a project's materials/cut-list takeoff.
  • Bids, invoices, expenses, and income entries you create, including amounts, descriptions, and dates.
  • Price-book entries: item descriptions, units, costs, dates, and optional supplier names you enter.
  • Photos, screenshots, PDFs, spreadsheets, links, and notes you choose to attach to a bid, invoice, expense, income entry, or price-book item.
  • A store item's URL, when you choose to paste one so the app can try to read its title and price automatically (see Section 4).

The app does not ask for your name, email address, or any account — it has no login of any kind.

2. Information Google AdMob may collect and process

Not applicable. Quaverta does not display ads and does not include the AdMob SDK or any other advertising SDK.

3. Advertising and personalised advertising

Not applicable. The app shows no advertising, personalised or otherwise.

4. Network access, device identifiers, and diagnostic data

The app does not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising services, and does not transmit an advertising identifier, your location, or diagnostic data anywhere. Two specific features do reach the network, only when you use them:

  • Store link lookup: if you paste a store item's URL into a bid line item, the app requests that exact page directly from the store's own website, to try to read its title and price automatically. The store (and any network it uses, such as a CDN) receives this request the same way it would from your device's web browser; the request carries none of your project, bid, invoice, expense, income, or price-book data.
  • On-device text recognition (OCR): photographing a receipt or price tag is read using Google's ML Kit text-recognition library, which runs on your device. The first time this feature is used, your device may download a small recognition-model file from Google's servers; after that, recognition runs entirely on-device with no further network access. No photo, receipt, or app data is ever sent to Google or anyone else as part of this.

Outside of those two cases, the app makes no network requests.

5. How information is used

Everything you enter is used solely to generate the materials list, cut list, bids, invoices, and price-book lookups the app displays and exports for you. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the app on your device.

6. Service providers and third-party sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party — the app has no server component of our own. The exceptions are all entirely under your control:

  • When you export a PDF or spreadsheet, the app hands that file to your device's own share sheet, and it goes only to whatever app or contact you personally choose there (for example, Mail, Messages, or Drive).
  • When you paste a store item's URL, that URL (and nothing else) is requested directly from the store's own website — see Section 4.
  • When you photograph a receipt or price tag, on-device text recognition may cause Google's servers to be contacted once, to download a recognition model — see Section 4. No app data is included in that request.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally in the app's own storage on your device. It stays there until you delete the relevant project, bid, invoice, expense, income entry, or price-book item within the app, or until you uninstall the app — which removes all of it immediately, since nothing exists anywhere else.

8. Children's privacy

Quaverta is a professional estimating tool for tradespeople and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. Because the app has no account and no analytics, it has no mechanism to identify or collect information about any user's age in the first place.

9. Security

Your project, bid, invoice, expense, income, and price-book data is never transmitted, so there is no app data in transit to protect. The two narrow network requests described in Section 4 (a pasted store URL, a one-time OCR model download) use standard HTTPS and carry no app data. Data at rest is protected by your device's own operating-system app sandboxing, the same as any other locally-stored app data.

10. User choices and controls

  • Delete any project, bid, invoice, expense, income entry, or price-book item at any time from within the app.
  • Deleting a project also deletes everything filed under it (structure, materials, cut list, bids, invoices, expenses, and income).
  • Revoke the app's camera permission at any time in your device's system settings — the app will simply be unable to attach new photos until you grant it again.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its data, since none of it is stored anywhere else.

11. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects or how it works changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
